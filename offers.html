STEP 1

Choose Your Website Type

5 distinct sub-offers
SUB-OFFER 01

Brand Website

A complete online presence that explains the whole business, builds credibility, presents services, and helps visitors get in touch. Covers Home, About, Services, Credentials, and Contact.

Who it's for: Clinics, healthcare, consultants, restaurants, agencies, established businesses getting their first proper site.
SUB-OFFER 02

Landing Page

Directs visitors toward one primary action instead of presenting the whole business. Built with conversion velocity, fast loading speed, and razor-sharp messaging.

Who it's for: Promoting one specific service or offer, consultants with a package, businesses running paid ads.
SUB-OFFER 03

Portfolio Website

Showcases capability through projects, case studies, and selected work. The work itself is the central reason the client gets hired.

Who it's for: Designers, photographers, architects, creative studios, freelancers, visual craftspeople.
SUB-OFFER 04

Website Redesign

Reviews an existing website's structure and content, then refreshes its design and mobile experience. Any migration work is agreed as part of the scope.

Who it's for: Established brands with outdated websites, poor mobile conversion rates, or slow performance.
SUB-OFFER 05

Custom Website Build

A bespoke website built around specific requirements that standard website types do not cover, such as custom calculator tools, member portals, or multi-market experiences.

Who it's for: Distinctive briefs, multi-platform ventures, businesses requiring custom application logic.
STEP 2

Choose Your Experience Level

Choose the interaction style that fits your content and audience, from clear and lightweight to story-driven.

LEVEL 1: ESSENTIAL ⚡

Clean, professional, straightforward

Clean layouts and typography, responsive design, lightweight interactions, and clear calls to action, with readability at the center.

Best suited for: Local businesses, clinics, consultants, clients who value clear information over heavy effects.
LEVEL 2: MOTION ✨

Expressive and interactive

Entrance animations, smooth transitions, interactive cards, animated visual elements, and micro-interactions that guide visitor attention.

Best suited for: Creative agencies, premium service businesses, studios wanting a memorable modern polish.
LEVEL 3: IMMERSIVE 🎬

Story-driven experience

Scroll-triggered sequences, layered visuals, pinned or staged sections, advanced interactions where moving through the site is part of the story.

Best suited for: High-end creative studios, distinctive product launches, brand storytelling.
