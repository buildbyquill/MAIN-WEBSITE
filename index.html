STEP 2: CHOOSE YOUR EXPERIENCE LEVEL

The Feel: How visitors interact with your website

LEVEL 1: ESSENTIAL ⚡

Clean, professional, straightforward

Clean layouts and typography, responsive design, lightweight interactions, and clear calls to action, with readability at the center.

Best suited for: Local businesses, clinics, consultants, clients who value clarity over heavy effects.
LEVEL 2: MOTION ✨

Expressive and interactive

Entrance animations, smooth transitions, hover physics, animated visual elements, custom interaction design that guides visitor attention.

Best suited for: Creative agencies, premium service businesses, studios wanting strong polish.
LEVEL 3: IMMERSIVE 🎬

Story-driven experience

Scroll-triggered sequences, layered visuals, pinned or staged sections, advanced interactions where moving through the site is part of the story.

Best suited for: High-end creative studios, distinctive product launches, visual storytelling.
